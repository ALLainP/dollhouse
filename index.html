How to Make a
Dollhouse

Would you like to take yourself up for a creative challenge? Or surprise your kids with a budget-friendly dream toy? Either way you would accomplish a man’s dream of building a house!

Jump to Instructions View materials
ABy Kuon Alina · Updated on November 4, 2026
DifficultyBeginner–Intermediate
Making time40-90 min
Approx. scale1 : 12
Main techniqueCut and glue

Overview

This beginner-friendly guide walks you through cutting, assembling and furnishing a dollhouse designed for imaginative play. Using pre-made templates with assembly hints this guide makes a regular dollhouse making process suitable for a child-parent play activity.

Before you begin: The dimensions and cut list below are a starter specification. Make a small test joint using your actual board first, since board thickness and compression vary by supplier.

01 / Preparation

Materials & tools

Gather everything before cutting. Tick items off as you prepare your workspace

02 / Downloads

Prepare your cutting templates

01

Print

Print the dollhouse details’ layouts on an A3 sized paper at 100%. Attach the printout named “Cardboard” to a cardboard sheet with masking tape, make sure the printout’s sides are aligned with the cardboard sheet. Leave the “Decor layout” to the side for now.

02

Cut and label the parts

Cut out all of the details along the thick black line. Do not cut along the thin line, instead transfer the thin line on the cardboard with a pencil, you will use them as guidance lines during the assembly stage. Mark the name of the details with a pencil as well, to avoid confusion later on.

Note: Each of the printouts has a scale bar at the bottom. If you are unsure whether the layouts have been printed successfully, measure the scale bar with a ruler. If the centimeters on the ruler match those on the paper - you have printed the correct size, if not - adjust the paper size to A3, and make sure the scale is set either to 100% or “Fit to paper”
Cutting tip: Cut with several light passes and keep the blade vertical. Use a fresh blade and a metal ruler.
03

Attach the decor

Grab the “Decor” printout and using scissors cut out the interior decor prints. Attach each one of them with a stick glue to its corresponding detail.

04 / Build the house

Step-by-step instructions

Keep the labelled panels grouped together.

01

Build the base

Starting from the 1st floor detail, attach the bottom of the back wall with a glue using the guidance lines on the printouts.

02

Attach the Porch and the Backyard

Then, attach the porch and backyard details. First, place the Backyard 1 and Porch 1 details, spread glue on both bottom and side attached to the back wall. Then place the Backyard 2 and Porch 2 details, spread glue on both the bottom and side attached to adjacent detail.

03

Place the 1st floor walls

Then, attach the right and left walls, make sure you spread the glue on both the bottom and the side attached to the back wall. Place the 1st floor interior wall on the guidance line on the Floor 1 spreading glue on both the bottom and the side attached to the back wall. Make sure the interior decor colors are aligned.

Make sure the green decor is facing this way

04

Fit in the 2nd floor and the balcony

Then, place the 2nd Floor detail, using the guidance lines on the Right wall, Left wall and Back wall spread the glue on 3 respective sides of the 2nd floor detail. Place the detail on top of the Inner wall 1 carefully sliding it tightly towards the back wall. Make sure that the decor is facing upwards. In a same manner fit in the Balcony detail as shown on the image.

05

Place the inner wall 2

Place the inner wall 2 on the guidance line on the Floor 2 detail. Spread glue on both the bottom and the side attached to the back wall.

06

Fit the roof

Finally, spread glue on the top rib of the right wall, left wall and the inner 2 wall, place both roof details on top of the walls as shown on the image. Add glue at the meeting point of 2 roof details as well. Wait approximately 10-15 minutes for the whole structure to dry.

05 / Finishing touches

Make it your own

Keep the core shell reusable. Add colour, paper, textiles and small details once the fit has been tested.

Add furniture and details

Using linked templates and tutorials below feel free to further decorate the house with hand-made furniture. Keep pieces movable and avoid tiny fragile details if the house will be handled often.

Test before the play

Check that all the parts have been glued thoroughly and reinforce parts with kraft masking tape where necessary. Verify the house stays stable during handling and only then proceed to use it for play.
